GLP-1 food tracker

A food tracker for GLP-1 medicines

Updated 2026-11-01

GLP-1 medicines such as semaglutide and tirzepatide often make meals smaller. Nutriva AI's GLP-1 mode helps you keep track of what you eat, especially protein, and remembers your dose day.

Not medical advice

Nutriva AI never suggests, changes or checks doses. For severe or lasting side effects, contact your prescriber. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

What GLP-1 mode does

  • Dose-day reminders on the daily or weekly schedule you enter.
  • Protein first: your protein goal stays in view, so smaller meals still count.
  • Side-effect check-ins: note how you feel in a few taps.
  • Optional coaching: turn on "Share with Marcus" and the AI coach takes your week into account when you ask what to eat. It's off by default.

Logging smaller meals fast

Snap a photo of a half-finished plate, scan a protein shake's barcode, or just say "half a chicken wrap". Nutriva AI estimates the calories and protein so you can see if you're on track for the day.

Questions

Is this app a replacement for my doctor's advice?

No. Nutriva AI isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. It never suggests, changes or checks doses. Your prescriber decides your treatment.

Why does protein matter on a GLP-1 medicine?

When appetite drops, meals get smaller and it gets harder to eat enough protein. Many people and their care teams track protein for this reason. Ask your doctor or dietitian what target is right for you.

Which medicines does GLP-1 mode support?

You can choose semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide (daily) or another GLP-1 medicine, with a daily or weekly schedule.

What happens to my medicine information?

It stays in your account. It's only sent to Marcus, the AI coach, if you turn on "Share with Marcus", which is off by default. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
