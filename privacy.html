1. Who We Are

Nutriva AI ("we", "us", "our") is the developer and publisher of the Nutriva AI mobile application available on the Apple App Store. We are responsible for determining how and why your personal data is processed.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at nutrivaaiteam@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you register.
  • Profile Data: Gender, date of birth, height, weight, activity level, dietary preferences, and health goals you enter during onboarding or at any time.
  • Nutrition & Food Data: Meal descriptions, food photos, barcode scans, calorie and macro entries, water intake, and fasting logs you record.
  • Body Measurements: Waist, chest, arms, hips, thighs, and neck measurements if you choose to log them.
  • GLP-1 Mode: If you turn on GLP-1 mode: the medicine you choose, your dose schedule and reminder time, and your side-effect check-ins (appetite, nausea, constipation, energy). This stays on your iPhone (see Section 5).
  • AI Coach Conversations: Messages you send to the AI coach (Marcus) and its replies are stored in your Nutriva account so the Coach can work, to protect the service from abuse, and so our team can check the safety and quality of its replies when needed. They are deleted when you delete your account (see Section 6).
  • Feedback & Support: Any information you provide when you contact us for support.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and app version.
  • Usage Data: Which screens you open and which features you use (for example "scanned a meal" or "sent a Coach message"), app version and device type, via PostHog — see Section 8. Never your food, health values, photos or chat text. You can turn this off in Settings › Share Usage & Crash Data.
  • Crash Reports: Stack traces, device model, iOS version, the screen you were on and app performance data if the app crashes, freezes or a request fails, via Sentry — see Section 8. No health or food data. You can turn this off in Settings › Share Usage & Crash Data.
  • IP Address: Collected by our backend (Supabase) for security and abuse prevention purposes.
  • Device identifier: If you apply a referral code, we store an identifier Apple assigns to Nutriva on your device (the identifier for vendor) with that referral, only to stop the same device from redeeming more than one referral. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is deleted with your account.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location (GPS coordinates).
  • We do not read your contacts, calendar, or messages.
  • We do not show ads in the app and we do not include any advertising SDK. To see which of our own ads work, we use only Apple's privacy-preserving attribution (SKAdNetwork and Apple Search Ads attribution), which reports results to us in aggregate and never identifies you (see Section 8).
  • We do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not show Apple's tracking prompt, and we do not use your health, food, weight, GLP-1 or chat data for advertising.
  • Our website (nutrivaai.com) does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Ever.

5. Health Data, Apple Health & GLP-1 Mode

Nutriva AI reads Apple Health (HealthKit) data only with your permission, and only for the data types you allow. Apple Health data is read on your device. Depending on what you allow, the app may read:

  • Activity: activity rings, steps, active energy and workouts
  • Sleep
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Blood oxygen
  • Body weight and body measurements
  • Dietary energy and macronutrient intake

The app uses this data to show your activity, sleep, readiness, stress and energy views and your weekly Health Review. With your permission, the app may also write nutrition data (calories, macros) and body weight back to Apple Health to keep your health records complete. You can change or withdraw these permissions at any time in the Health app or your device Settings.

Important: your health data is never sold or used for advertising Data from Apple Health is used only to provide the app's features to you. It is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with data brokers or insurers.

AI Coach: only when you use the AI Coach and your question is about your health, Nutriva can send a short summary of the relevant numbers with your question, such as your weight trend, steps, active energy, sleep, resting heart rate, HRV or blood oxygen, so Marcus can answer (see Section 6). Apple Health data is not sent to AI providers for anything else.

This use of HealthKit data complies with Apple's HealthKit guidelines.

GLP-1 mode

GLP-1 mode is optional and off until you turn it on. If you use it, the medicine you choose, your dose schedule and reminder time, and your side-effect check-ins are sensitive health data. We use them only to show your dose-day card and reminder, your protein target and your check-in history in the app.

  • Where it is stored: only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage, in a separate file for each Nutriva account. It is not synced to our servers and is excluded from iCloud and device backups, so it is not restored on a new iPhone. Dose-day reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS.
  • AI Coach: shared with Marcus only if you turn on "Share with Marcus" in GLP-1 mode (it is off by default). When it is on, your medicine, where you are in your dose week and your most recent check-in are sent with your questions to the AI Coach (processed by OpenAI, see Section 6). You can turn it off at any time.
  • Never sold or used for advertising: GLP-1 data is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sent to PostHog, Sentry or any advertising service. Usage analytics may record that a GLP-1 mode screen or check-in was used, but never which medicine, your schedule or your check-in answers.
  • Export and deletion: your check-ins are included when you export your data. Signing out keeps the file on that iPhone for when you sign back in; deleting your account deletes it.

6. AI Features

Nutriva AI uses AI models from OpenAI (GPT models) to analyze food photos, labels, menus and meal descriptions, to power the AI Coach (Marcus), and to write health insights and weekly reviews. If OpenAI is temporarily unavailable, food and meal estimates may be processed by Google (Gemini API) instead.

What is sent

  • Food analysis: the photo or description you submit and your app language. We do not send your name or email.
  • AI Coach: your message, the recent conversation, and the context Marcus needs to answer: your first name, goal, weight and goal weight, height, age, sex, activity level, diet preference, today's food and water totals, recent averages, recent meal names and anything you asked Marcus to remember. When your question is about your health, a short summary of relevant Apple Health numbers is included (see Section 5). If you turned on "Share with Marcus" in GLP-1 mode, your medicine, where you are in your dose week and your most recent check-in are included too (off by default). Email, phone numbers and card numbers typed into a message are removed before sending.

You choose whether to allow this: the app asks before the first AI request, and you can change it in Settings at any time. AI features are not available without that permission; everything else keeps working.

OpenAI and Google process these requests under their API terms and, by default, do not use API data to train their models. Your Coach conversations are stored in your Nutriva account so the service works and is protected from abuse, and they are deleted when you delete your account.

AI-generated responses are based on statistical models and may contain inaccuracies. Never use AI food estimates or coaching advice for medical purposes, including insulin dosing. Marcus is an AI, not a doctor or dietitian.

Reported replies and safety checks

If you report a reply from the AI Coach, the reply, the question you asked just before it, the reason you chose, your app version and language are stored with your account so our team can review them. Reports are deleted after 90 days, or earlier if you delete your account.

Before your message reaches the AI, Nutriva checks it on our server for signs of a medical emergency or self-harm. When it finds one, it shows a fixed message pointing to emergency help instead of an AI answer. These checks keep no copy of your message.

Nutriva AI offers an auto-renewing subscription, Nutriva Pro. New accounts, created with version 2.1 of the app or later, start with Nutriva Pro (a free trial or introductory offer may apply for eligible new subscribers); accounts created before then keep their free plan. Subscription status is verified through RevenueCat, which receives your anonymised device identifier and purchase receipt from the app store to confirm your entitlements. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or health data.

For subscription management, cancellations, and refunds, please use your device's subscription management settings:

  • iPhone: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions

Refund requests are handled by Apple according to its refund policies. Deleting your Nutriva account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it separately in your Apple ID settings.

8. Analytics, Crash Reporting & Ad Measurement

To find and fix bugs and to see which features are used, Nutriva AI sends crash and error reports to Sentry and usage events to PostHog. They carry your random account ID, never your name, email or phone number, and never your food, photos, Apple Health data, weights or Coach messages. Apple Health data is never used for analytics. Neither tool is used for advertising or to track you across other apps and websites.

Ad measurement (Apple only). To learn which of our ads bring new users, the app uses Apple's own privacy-preserving attribution: SKAdNetwork, where Apple tells an ad network, in aggregate and with a delay, how many installs from its ads were opened or started a subscription, and Apple Search Ads attribution (AdServices), which tells us which Apple Search Ads campaign led to an install. Neither identifies you, neither uses your advertising identifier (IDFA), and neither is tracking, so the app does not show Apple's tracking prompt. No health, food, weight, GLP-1, photo or chat data is ever part of it.

Your choice. Usage events (PostHog) are off in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland until you allow them; elsewhere they are on by default. Crash reports are on until you turn them off. Turn all of this off any time in the app: Profile › Settings › Share Usage & Crash Data. Apple may also share anonymous crash reports with us if you allow it in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

9. Camera, Microphone & Photo Library

The app requests access to these device capabilities for the following specific purposes:

  • Camera: To capture photos of food for AI-powered calorie analysis, and to scan barcodes.
  • Microphone: To enable voice food logging — you can describe a meal by speaking. Audio is processed on-device or sent to the AI model for transcription only; raw audio is not stored.
  • Photo Library: To allow you to select an existing photo of food for AI analysis, or to set your profile picture.

These permissions are optional. The app will function without them, but some features will be unavailable. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device Settings.

11. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • On-device storage: Data on your device is kept in the app's private storage (the iOS app sandbox), protected by iOS, and synced to your Nutriva account.
  • Data in transit: All communication between the app and our servers uses TLS 1.2+ encryption.
  • Cloud storage: Account and sync data is stored in Supabase, which is hosted on infrastructure with SOC 2 Type II certification.
  • Access controls: Only you can access your data. We use row-level security policies to ensure users cannot access other users' data.
  • No plaintext passwords: Passwords are hashed and never stored in plaintext.

Despite our best efforts, no method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to nutrivaaiteam@gmail.com.
