Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how Nutriva AI handles "consumer health data" under the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law. It adds to our Privacy Policy.

1. Consumer health data we collect

  • Food, calorie, macro, water and fasting logs, and food photos you add
  • Weight, height, age, sex, activity level, body measurements, health goals and dietary preferences
  • With your permission, Apple Health data: activity, workouts, sleep, heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, weight and nutrition
  • GLP-1 mode: your medicine, dose schedule and side-effect check-ins (kept only on your iPhone)
  • What you tell the AI Coach (Marcus) about your health, and any replies you report

2. Why we collect and use it

To provide what you ask for: calorie and nutrition tracking, targets, progress and health views, reminders, food analysis and the AI Coach. We also use it to keep the service secure and to prevent abuse, and to check the safety and quality of AI replies. We never sell it or use it for advertising.

3. Where it comes from

From you, from your device, and from Apple Health when you allow it.

4. What we share, and with whom

We share only what's needed to run the app, with service providers that process it for us: Supabase (cloud storage and accounts) and, when you use AI features, OpenAI and Google (food analysis and the AI Coach). See Section 4 of our Privacy Policy for details. We have no affiliates that receive this data. We may also disclose it if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

5. Your rights

You can:

  • Confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data (we do not sell it)
  • Access it, and get a list of the third parties and affiliates we've shared it with
  • Withdraw your consent
  • Have it deleted, including from our service providers and from our backups within 30 days

You can delete your account and data in the app under Profile → Account → Delete account, withdraw Apple Health permission in iOS Settings, and turn off AI features in Coach settings. For anything else, email nutrivaaiteam@gmail.com with the subject "Consumer Health Data Request". We reply within 45 days, and may ask you to verify your identity. Requests are free, up to twice a year.

6. If we say no

You can appeal by replying to our decision within 30 days. We answer appeals within 45 days. If your appeal is denied, you can complain to the Washington State Attorney General or the Nevada Attorney General.
