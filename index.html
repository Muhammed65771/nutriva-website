Features

Everything in one calm, fast app.

Nutriva AI 2.0 is a native app for iPhone and Apple Watch. Here's what it does today.

Log food fast

Use whatever's quickest in the moment.

  • AI photo scan of a meal
  • Barcode scan and nutrition-label scan
  • Describe a meal by typing or by voice
  • A verified offline food catalog of about 2,000 foods
  • Recent foods and meal templates

Calories, macros and AI results are estimates and can be wrong. Check labels and adjust portions.

Marcus, your AI coach

Ask about your food, your goals and your health data, and get a clear, friendly answer.

Marcus is an AI, not a doctor or dietitian, and doesn't give medical advice.

Works with Apple Health

See your day in one place, using only the data you allow.

  • Activity rings, steps and workouts
  • Sleep, heart rate and HRV
  • Readiness, stress and energy
  • Weight and body measurements

Barcodes you can trust

Nutrition numbers from a barcode are checked across several food databases. When they disagree, the app asks you to confirm with the nutrition label instead of guessing.

Private where possible

On supported iPhones, meal descriptions are read on the device first. Photos are analysed in the cloud. No ads and no tracking.

Works offline

No signal? Scans wait safely on your phone and are analysed as soon as you're back online.

Made for everyone

  • Dynamic Type
  • VoiceOver
  • Reduce Motion
  • Dark and light mode

And more

  • Strength logging
  • Weekly Health Review
  • Weekly meal plan: seven days of simple meals sized to your calories and protein, with a shopping list
  • Widgets
  • Siri, Shortcuts and the Action Button
  • Apple Watch app

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a subscription?

Accounts created on or after 20 October 2026 need Nutriva Pro (Yearly or Monthly). Accounts created before that date keep their free plan.

How does the free trial work?

Eligible new subscribers can start the Yearly plan with a free trial. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it becomes a paid yearly subscription charged to your Apple ID. If you cancel during the trial, you aren't charged.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Nutriva AI → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel the subscription.

How do I restore my purchases?

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Restore Purchases (you can also restore from the subscription screen). Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

What happens to my data if my subscription ends?

Your data is kept in your account. You can export it (Profile → Account → Export Data) or delete your account at any time.

Which AI does Nutriva AI use, and what is sent?

Food photos, typed and spoken meal descriptions and Coach messages are sent to AI providers (OpenAI, with Google Gemini as a backup for food estimates) to calculate nutrition and answer you. These providers don't use API data to train their models by default. The app asks before the first AI request. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Is Nutriva AI medical advice?

No. Nutriva AI is not a medical device and doesn't provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Calories, macros and AI results are estimates. Marcus is an AI, not a doctor or dietitian. Talk to a doctor before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant, have diabetes, an eating disorder or another condition.

Who can use Nutriva AI?

You must be at least 13 years old, or 16 where local law requires it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete account. This removes your data from our servers. See step-by-step instructions.
